🚀 4-Phase Operational Workflow (From Ingest to Demob)

          
This tool bridges 911 dispatch databases and CalTopo to ensure 100% accountability of
            residential structures during high-stress evacuation incidents:


          

            

              
1. Ingest Master List (ICP)

              
Load 911 parcel or address CSV
                in Step 1. Map Latitude, Longitude, and Address. Specify an optional Street
                  Name column so CalTopo automatically generates individual folders per street.

            


            

              
2. Build & Deploy CalTopo Map

              
Click "Build CalTopo Map
                  File" and download the GeoJSON. Import this file into your team's CalTopo incident map (`Import →
                GeoJSON`). Share the map bookmark/link with field team leaders.

            


            

              
3. Field Search & Evac Tracking

              
Searchers open CalTopo on mobile
                devices. As they knock on doors, they tap the structure waypoint and change its icon to reflect status
                (Evacuating, Refused, Needs Assistance, etc.).

            


            

              
4. Reconcile & Triage (ICP)

              
Periodically export GeoJSON from
                CalTopo (`Export → GeoJSON`) and drop it into Step 2. The dashboard reconciles field
                marks against your master CSV and flags unvisited structures.

            

          

        


        
        

          
🗺️ CalTopo Field Setup & Sync Guide


          
How to Import into CalTopo
            (Incident Command Post):

          
    
            
  1. Download the GeoJSON file from Step 1 of this tool.
    2. 
            
  2. In your CalTopo incident map, click Import in the left sidebar menu (or Add ➔
                Import).
    3. 
            
  3. Select your downloaded .geojson file.
    4. 
            
  4. Confirm all folders and points are checked, then click Import.
    5. 
            
  5. Under map settings, copy the Share Map URL or generate a QR code for searchers to scan onto
              their mobile devices.
    6. 
          


          
How Field Responders Update
            Waypoints on Mobile:

          
    
            
  1. Open the CalTopo Mobile App (available on iOS and Android) and open the incident map.
    2. 
            
  2. Pre-download offline map layers before departing the command post in case cell coverage
              is lost.
    3. 
            
  3. Upon arriving at a residence, tap the structure's waypoint on the map.
    4. 
            
  4. Tap Edit (pencil icon), tap the icon symbol/color, and select the appropriate
              evacuation icon (e.g., green Evacuating, red Needs Assistance, or orange
              Refused).
            
    5. 
            
  5. Add any critical notes (e.g., "2 elderly occupants, 1 dog evacuated") into the waypoint
              comment/description field and tap Save.
    6. 
          


          
How to Export Field Updates for
            Reconciliation:

          
    
            
  1. In CalTopo, click Export in the top menu bar.
    2. 
            
  2. Select GeoJSON format.
    3. 
            
  3. Ensure the evacuation folders are checked and click Export to download the file.
    4. 
            
  4. Drop that file into Step 2 of this tool to instantly see updated counts and unvisited
              homes.

🏷️ Symbology & Reconciliation Glossary

          
Understanding the difference between reconciliation categories is critical for life-safety accountability:
          


          

            

              
                

                  Reconciliation Category
                  What it Means
                  Operational Action Required
                

              
              
                

                  
                    ⚠️ UNVISITED (Master Only) / Missing / Diff
                  
                  
                    This address exists in your initial Master CSV roster, but was completely missing
                    from the CalTopo export. (Not plotted, deleted, or coordinates drastically changed).
                  
                  
                    High Priority: Verify why this house is missing. Dispatch field teams to check if
                    the residence was bypassed or accidentally left off the field map.
                  
                

                

                  
                    🔵 Unchecked / Not Visited
                  
                  
                    The structure is plotted on the CalTopo map, but still carries the default initial blue pin. Field
                    teams have not yet logged an evacuation status.
                  
                  
                    Assign to incoming field teams for initial door-to-door evacuation notification.
                  
                

                

                  
                    🌱 Added in Field (New Structure)
                  
                  
                    A structure waypoint added directly by field teams that does not match any row in the original
                    master roster (e.g. unrecorded ADU, cabin, or RV trailer).
                  
                  
                    Cross-reference with GIS and add resident details to the incident record.
                  
                

                

                  
                    📍 ICS / Incident Point
                  
                  
                    Non-residential markers (Roadblocks, Staging Areas, Helispots, Landing Zones) placed on the map.
                  
                  
                    Automatically isolated so incident landmarks do not inflate residential evacuation numbers.
                  
                

              
            

          


          
CalTopo Default Evacuation
            Icon Standard:

          

            

              Evacuating / Cleared (#00a651)

              Occupants have evacuated or are leaving now.
                Structure safe & clear.
            

            

              Needs Assistance (#fff200)

              Urgent: Resident requires evacuation
                transport, medical assistance, or EMS.
            

            

              Sheltering In Place (#f7941d)

              Occupant staying in structure (either by
                choice or escape routes cut off).
            

            

              Refused Evacuation (#ff914d)

              Occupant was contacted and informed of danger,
                but refused to depart.
            

            

              No Response / Followup (#a7a9ac)

              No answer at door; team could not verify if
                occupants are home or gone.
            

            

              Unattended Animal (#a97c50)

              Livestock or pets left behind requiring Animal
                Control / rescue.
            

            

              Residence Not Accessible (#d32f2f)

              Driveway blocked by locked gate, downed trees,
                fire, or flooding.
            

            

              No Residence Exists (#424242)

              Vacant lot or commercial parcel; no
                residential home present.
            

          

        


        
        

          
❓ Incident FAQ & Troubleshooting


          

            Q: What happens if my command post laptop shuts down or the browser tab closes?
            
All loaded data, column
              mappings, and triage reviews are securely cached in your local browser memory with a 48-hour sliding inactivity
              timer. Every interaction automatically refreshes this 48-hour window, so ongoing multi-day operations are
              never lost. Incident data is only automatically cleared after 48 consecutive hours of complete inactivity.

          


          

            Q: What coordinate formats are accepted?
            
The tool expects
              coordinates in standard Decimal Degrees WGS84 (e.g. Latitude: 39.7592, Longitude:
              -121.6219). If your coordinates are in MGRS, USNG, UTM, or Degrees/Minutes, convert them to
              decimal degrees prior to import.
            

          


          

            Q: What if field teams lose cellular service in the canyon / backcountry?
            
The CalTopo mobile app
              operates fully offline with internal GPS. Before leaving the Command Post, searchers must open CalTopo,
              tap Download Offline Map, and cache the incident area. Status edits will save locally on their
              phones and sync back to the team map when they regain cellular or ICP Wi-Fi coverage.

          


          

            Q: How do I share updates with EOC / EMO / EMCR / ECC / AHJ?
            
In Step
                2, use the "Download Filtered CSV" (e.g. for only "Needs Assistance" cases) or
              "Download All CSV" to provide an official spreadsheet to the Planning Section Chief or EOC for
              ICS-204 assignment documentation.
